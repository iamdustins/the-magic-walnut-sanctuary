[ CULTIVATION ]
[ 01: lighting ]

Bright Indirect Foot-Candles

Variegated white sectors contain zero chlorophyll and cannot convert light. Give them 400–800 foot candles of indirect sun or a 24W Sansi LED 12" away. Low light causes white tissue to brown off.

[ 02: hydration ]

Chunky Substrate Cycle

Never keep your Albo sitting in wet muck. Water thoroughly until water drains freely through the pot bottom, then allow top 50–60% of the chunky mix to dry before watering again.

[ 03: soil recipe ]

Sanctuary Aroid Mix

Our mix: 40% coarse pine bark nuggets, 25% chunky perlite (#3/#4), 20% coconut husk chunks, 10% worm castings, 5% horticultural charcoal. Roots breathe freely with maximum aeration.

[ 04: browning ]

White Tissue Preservation

White browning is almost always caused by uneven root moisture or lack of airflow. Maintain 50–65% ambient humidity with a small oscillating fan. Do not mist the white leaves directly.

[ 05: acclimation ]

Arrival Protocol

Upon unboxing, do not repot immediately. Place the specimen in medium ambient light for 7 days. Give it a gentle drink if the moss or substrate feels dry to the touch.

[ 06: propagation ]

Node & Aerial Root Care

Always preserve the aerial root on each node. Root in damp stratum or perlite/chunky moss with gentle bottom heat until secondary feeder roots reach 2–3 inches.
