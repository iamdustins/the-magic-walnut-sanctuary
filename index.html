SANCTUARY FIELD MANUAL • WRITTEN BY DUSTIN

🌿 THE MONSTERA ALBO CARE GUIDE

1,000+ SPECIMENS GROWN & SOLD
"These plants are iconic for two reasons: half because they are beautiful, and half because they are a mystery. I've sold over 1,000 of these plants — and I've killed my fair share along the way. Everything in this guide is based on personal experience: what has worked for me, what hasn't, and the lessons learned from both. Use me as a resource. Happy PLANTING!" — Dustin
1. Sanctuary Conditions

HOW YOUR PLANT WAS GROWN

  • • Temp: 50°–75°F (Cooler months) to 70°–90°F (Summer).
  • • Humidity: Consistently around 75%.
  • • Light: 14–16 hours of daylight daily.
  • • Golden Rule: Watch videos, read up, and find your own rhythm with your plant!
2. Leafless Nodes (No Roots)

50/50 PERLITE & MOSS

  • • The Mix: 50/50 mix of Perlite and Sphagnum Moss.
  • • The Setup: Place node & mix in an airtight container to lock in high humidity.
  • • Air Exchange: Keep closed mostly, but open periodically to let it breathe—air exchange is vital!
3. Unrooted Leaf Cuttings

BUBBLER / AEROPONICS

  • • Method: Water with a bubbler (air stone) or aeroponics system.
  • • Temp: Keep above 70°F to stimulate root growth.
  • • Light: 14–16 hrs bright light with the green leaf surface facing the light.
4. Rooted Plants & Stability

THE "NO MOVE" RULE

  • • Substrate: Very light, airy, chunky soil mix (roots need oxygen!).
  • • Placement: Southern/Southwestern window indoors, or morning sun / shade cloth outdoors.
  • • 2 Keys to Success: (1) Consistent watering rhythm & (2) DON'T MOVE IT once happy and growing!
GROWER'S NOTEBOOK • PAGE #01

FIRST 7 DAYS AFTER UNBOXING

  • 1. DO NOT REPOT IMMEDIATELY: Let your new plant acclimate to your room's humidity and light for at least 10–14 days in its sanctuary cup.
  • 2. THE "NO MOVE" RULE: Once you place your plant in bright indirect light and it starts pushing growth, leave it right there—stability is everything.
  • 3. ASK DUSTIN ANYTIME: Not sure if your node or cutting looks happy? Post a photo in The Hobby Botanist FB Group!
